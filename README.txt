Fatimah Birthday Page
=====================

How to open:
1. Unzip this folder
2. Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome / Safari / Edge)

What's included:
- Age splash screen (18 → 19) — tap anywhere to continue (no auto-dismiss)
- Soft looping background music (from your video) starts on that first tap, at low volume
- Hero photo of Fatimah
- New soft banner photo (the outdoor selfie)
- 11 memory photos in the gallery
- 16 video clips in a dual sliding banner
  • Top strip scrolls to the right
  • Bottom strip scrolls to the left
  • All clips autoplay muted in the banners
  • Tap / click any clip → full-screen popup with sound & controls
- Personal letter with the full birthday message spread out
- 5 hidden gifts (🎁) scattered around the page
  • Tap them to reveal sweet little notes
  • Counter at the bottom tracks how many you’ve found

To add more later:
Edit script.js → CONFIG.photos or CONFIG.videos arrays
and drop the new files into the media/ folder.
